How it works Every job follows the same steps
01

Tell us what you need

Just moved in and want to rekey? Lock is sticking? Want a deadbolt added? Tell us the problem and how many doors are involved. If you know the lock brand (check the face - it usually says Schlage or Kwikset), that helps.

02

We check your doors and hardware

On-site, we look at your door alignment, frame condition, and current lock quality. A new lock on a warped door fails fast - we catch that before recommending anything. You get honest options: repair, rekey, or replace.

03

You pick the option that fits

We explain what each option involves. Rekeying is cheapest. Repair keeps your hardware. Replacement gives you better security. Your call - no pressure, no upselling.

04

We do the work and test everything

Keys turn smoothly, deadbolts throw fully, latches catch on their own, smart locks connect to your phone. We test from both sides of every door before considering the job done.

Common questions

Residential Locksmith FAQ

Should I rekey or replace my locks?

Rekey when the lock works but old keys should stop working, such as after moving in or losing a key. Replace damaged, loose, weak, or outdated hardware. We inspect the lock first and recommend the lower-scope option when rekeying solves it.

What are bump-proof or pick-resistant locks?

Pick-resistant and bump-resistant locks use added pin, sidebar, or key-control features to resist common attack methods. Brands like Medeco and Mul-T-Lock can be strong options for front doors and visible exterior entries.

Which lock options are safer for homes with small children?

For homes with children, interior thumb-turns help preserve quick exit, deadbolt covers can reduce curious lock use, and smart locks with auto-lock can prevent doors from staying unlocked. We can also install chain guards at adult height.

What grade of deadbolt should I use for my home?

Grade 1 and Grade 2 deadbolts are common exterior-door choices. We inspect the door, frame, and strike plate before recommending hardware.
