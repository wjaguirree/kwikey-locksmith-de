What affects the price

  • Lock type - standard, high-security, smart, or commercial
  • Service complexity - lockout vs. full rekey vs. installation
  • Parts needed - replacement hardware, transponder chips, cylinders
  • Location and timing - distance and service hours

Every job is different. We assess the actual lock, door, or vehicle on-site and explain what's involved before approved work begins.
